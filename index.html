Your people

Met at: your age when they came into your life. Use 0 for people who were there from the start.

Closeness: very close lines run right beside yours. When a very close person drifts apart, their line crosses yours and leaves from the opposite side. Close and distant people leave from the side they came in.

Left your life? Type the age in the second box. Leave it empty if they are still here.

Tick "Passed away" if they died. Their line ends and their star rises into the sky. If you only drifted apart, leave it unticked. Their line slowly slides off the side of the screen.

Nothing you type leaves this page. Only anonymous play counts are recorded.
